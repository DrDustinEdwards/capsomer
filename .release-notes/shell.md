# Shell

- More sheet markup: the dialog is now the shared dialog. Replace `<dialog class="cap-shell-more" ...><div class="cap-shell-more-card"><h2 class="cap-shell-more-title">...` with `<dialog class="cap-dialog cap-shell-more" data-cap="dialog" data-placement="bottom" id="cap-more" aria-labelledby="cap-more-title">`, a `.cap-dialog-header` holding `<h2 class="cap-dialog-title" id="cap-more-title">`, a `.cap-dialog-body` holding the `nav` and list, and a corner `<button class="cap-btn cap-dialog-close" data-variant="quiet" data-icon-only data-cap-part="close" aria-label="Close">`. The footer Close (`.cap-shell-more-foot`, `data-cap-part="more-close"`) is gone, as are `.cap-shell-more-card`, `.cap-shell-more-title` and `.cap-shell-more-foot`. The sheet is a bottom sheet (`data-placement="bottom"`). The React `<Shell>` does this itself; its props (`tabs`, `more`, `moreLabel`, `moreIcon`, `railFoot`, `status`, `actions`, `renderLink`, `collapsed`, `onCollapsedChange`, `prefKey`, `navLabel`, `brand`, `brandHref`) are unchanged.
- Look: menu items are `--control` tall (32 px compact, 36 comfortable, 44 on touch) with a 16 px icon (was 30 px and 14 px); the current item is the accent tint with `--accent-text` (was the lit surface with a hairline); a count is a small pill, red or amber on its soft tint (was bare text). Apps that overrode `.cap-shell-rail a` sizes should recheck.
- Collapsed rail: names show as an inverted tooltip with an arrow; the collapsed rail no longer scrolls (`overflow: visible`) so the tooltip is not clipped.
- New, optional: `.cap-shell-group` and `.cap-shell-group-label` for groups in the rail; `group` on a React `ShellEntry`.
- New: `readDensity`, `applyDensity`, `applyStoredDensity` and `DENSITY_PREF` exported from `shell.ts`; `data-density` on `<html>` remembered under `cap-density`.
- `readPref`, `writePref`, `setRail`, `toggleRail`, `openMore`, `closeMore`, `enhance` keep their names and signatures. `enhance()` no longer needs the dialog's own `enhance()` for the More sheet.
- The theme switch and the skip link, the top bar, `.cap-shell-settings` and the rail's hint classes keep their markup.
