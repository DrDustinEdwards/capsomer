Rest

The spring survey covered twelve sites along the creek, each visited once a week from March to May.

Counts rose through April and fell in the first week of May, when the water was highest.

Two sites were dry for most of March and are left out of the totals.

The full counts, by site and by week, are in the annual report.

Next season adds three sites upstream and moves the visits to Tuesdays.
