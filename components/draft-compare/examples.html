Webmentions arrive as small notes from other sites. Each one says who linked to a post and where. The queue shows them oldest first.

Moderation is a single decision per mention. Approve it, and it appears under the post. Hide it, and nothing is shown. A hidden mention can be restored later.

Every mention keeps the address of the page that sent it. The address is shown in full, never shortened. A mention from a site that has sent ten before is marked as familiar. A mention from a new site is marked as new. The mark is a word and a shape, never only a colour.

The agent that sorts the queue never approves anything on its own. It only suggests an order and gives a reason for each suggestion. A suggestion is a note beside the mention, not a decision.
