States and kinds with a mark of their own

The tone sets the color; the shape names the state (queued, unknown, blocked) or the kind of item (a question, a pull request, a report, a proposal, a site that is down). The quiet gray, data-tone="muted", is for a state that is neither good nor bad.

A phrase that wraps

A hold reason is a sentence, so it wraps in its own column (data-wrap) and the glyph stays by its first line.

Held until a person approves the change to the billing settings for example.com

A glyph on its own

A status icon beside a name, with no word of its own, takes its tone's color (data-tone on the glyph). The name beside it, or a hidden word, says the state.

  • example.com is up
  • api.example.com is down

Accent and series badges

The accent variant is a category that is not a state, with no glyph; data-family gives it another family's accent. A series dot colors a label a person made, the word beside it carrying the meaning.

Win-back Win-back, in the purple family

Tag badges

A tag is set in the site's tag face (--tag-*): the tone's tint with an edge in its own color, a quiet hairline label with no tone, or a family's accent. These set mono, upper case and no glyph, as Foxhound does.
