- **Removed (unused; no app imports them):** `needCount`, `worstSeverity` and `severityTone` (`capsomer/behavior/attention-list`); `activeCount`, `facetCounts` and `summaryText` (`capsomer/behavior/catalog`; `markdownCell` and `catalogParams` are no longer exported); `useConfirmAction` and the `ConfirmAsk` type (`capsomer/react/confirm-dialog`); `countMatches` (`capsomer/confirm-server`); `csvFileProblem` (`capsomer/behavior/chart-builder`; the dialog checks a chosen file with the drop zone's `validateFiles`, so its refusal reads "data.txt: type not allowed. This takes CSV." / "big.csv: too big at 300 kB. The limit is 200 kB.").
- **Removed: the confirm dialog's re-exports.** `capsomer/behavior/confirm-dialog` no longer re-exports `errorText`, `isBackdropClick`, `isBusy`, `rememberOpener` and `returnFocus` (import them from `capsomer/behavior/dialog`) or `wordMatches` (from `capsomer/confirm-server`). `capsomer/react/command-menu` no longer re-exports `useCommandHost`: import it from `capsomer/react/command-host`. `Command` gains `of?` (a Recent entry's original id).
- **Checklist run: Stop asks in the confirm dialog** ("Discard this run?", focus on Keep going), not an inline row; `.cap-run-confirm` is removed, and a page that runs checklists loads `confirm-dialog.css` and `dialog.css`. With no `data-run-noun` the button says "Run this checklist" again (it said "Run this undefined"), and the summary's ids are made per run, so two runs on a page do not share them.
- **Ambient: the equalizer's bars take their heights and timings.** The per-bar rules matched the container, not the bars.
- **AI sample: the button is `aria-busy`, never `disabled`, while the call runs**, so focus stays on it. React's `busyLabel` is now a string, said to a screen reader while `Button` keeps its words under a spinner; the React form carries `data-cap="ai-sample"` and `data-ai-sample-generate`. The title and field labels are `.cap-eyebrow[data-variant="mono"]` (`.cap-ai-sample-title` is removed), and the live dot no longer pulses.
- **Annotated document (React): the view and the lit note are React state**; the root no longer carries `data-cap="annotated"` and needs no `enhance()`. `name` defaults to a made-up id, so two documents on a page do not share a radio group. `bring` is exported from `capsomer/behavior/annotated-document`.
- **Attention list: each urgency is a list item holding its heading and its own `ul.cap-need-rows`**, so a list's count is its rows, never the headings (HTML contract). The `l` key has the typing, IME and repeat guards every list key has.
- **Approval queue, mentions list and moderation queue share `queueKey(e, list)`** (new, `capsomer/behavior/row-list`) for j, k and their guards; the approval queue's decisions are pending `Button`s.
- **Config form: focus goes back to the opener when it closes**; the hint is a warning `Alert` (`.cap-config-hint` is removed; needs `banner.css`).
- **Collab editor: deleting a comment asks first** in the confirm dialog; Edit starts from the comment as it is now; the suggestions' buttons name whose change they act on; an unreadable room message is said in the notice line.
- **Chart builder: a failed chart check shows its error** in a warning alert instead of nothing. **Chart frame:** the data preview is a `.cap-card` (needs `card.css`), and `countsByYearHtml` passes one row per year with its count.
- **Contact card: each card is `.cap-card .cap-contact-card`** (needs `card.css`; HTML contract), and its links' target is `var(--target)`. **Consent:** `.cap-permissions-heading` is drawn as the mono eyebrow (add `cap-eyebrow` and `data-variant="mono"`). **Annotated figure:** the key's number is the mono eyebrow too, and the leaders draw on the motion tokens.
- **Card: a disabled choice card is drawn as a disabled field** (dashed edge, sunken ground, muted words) instead of at 0.6 opacity; focus offsets use `--focus-offset`.
- **Carousel (React): the root carries `data-cap="carousel"`**; the markers no longer animate their width. **Calculator (React):** each field is a `Field` and Calculate a `Button`. **AI engine settings:** the table is `Table`, and an override's date is a `Time`. **ComboboxFreeText** no longer accepts the `searchHint` it ignored.
- **Admin shell: `data-cap-collapse-key` is documented**, and the collapse and jump keys use the shortcuts' `isTyping`.
